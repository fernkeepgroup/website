BeddyPals questions

Does BeddyPals need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. Buying Premium goes through your app store, which needs a connection. If you subscribe, BeddyPals checks in with your store now and then; after about six weeks offline, Premium waits until it can check again.

Where are the puppets and stories we make?

On your device, and in your device's own backup if you've switched backup on, so they can come back on a new or reset device. They're never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them from the device.

Why doesn't the app read the story aloud?

That's on purpose. BeddyPals shows the words a page at a time, and you tell or sing them in your own voice.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions renew until you turn them off in the app store you subscribed through. If one ends, you keep everything in the free set.

Privacy and terms

Our apps are made for families with young children. BeddyPals has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, asks for no device permissions, and keeps what you make on your tablet.

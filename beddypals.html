BeddyPals

Bedtime Puppet Adventures

A little bedtime theater for you to tell stories in. Pick a world, bring on up to three friendly puppets, and do the voices yourself. Your child gets a puppet face to poke and giggle at, while your fingers work the controls around the edges.

Works offline

No Wi-Fi needed at bedtime.

You tell the story. BeddyPals sets the stage.

Puppeteering

Tap, and the puppet talks.

Tap a puppet and it comes up close, filling the screen. Tap the edge controls for a syllable, hold for a looong word, and choose from 8 faces and 14 moves, from a high five to a sneeze. The face is your child's to poke, and a poke gets a blink.

Worlds

30 worlds, from the ocean to the stars.

Sail to Pirate Cove, stomp through Dinosaur Valley or ride the Night Train. Worlds sit on cozy shelves with your favorites first.

Stories and songs

Stuck for a story? Tell one a page at a time.

Six tales and six classic songs, from Goldilocks to Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star. The words appear in big lettering, a glowing ring shows little fingers what to tap, and the puppets act it out. BeddyPals never reads aloud: you tell it or sing it, in your own voice.

Make your own puppet

A puppet that looks like your child.

Make up to ten puppets: a child, baby, parent, grandparent, teddy bear, bunny or owl, each with their own skin tone, hair, glasses and pajamas. No photos, no camera, and what you make stays with your family.

Make your own story

Craft your own adventure.

Make up to ten stories of your own. Pick six pages from the worlds, with a bedroom last, give each one a title and your own words, and stand up to three puppets and a few props where they begin. Then tell it a page at a time, just like the built-in tales.

Goodnight

Say goodnight, and the light eases down.

Hold the little Zzz cloud and the puppets yawn and drift into bed. The blanket comes up and the screen settles into a warm, dim nightlight, with a sleep timer, a screen lock for little fingers, and a switch to turn motion off.

What BeddyPals keeps, and what it doesn't

Read the privacy policy
  • No ads, no analytics, no accounts. The only outside service the app talks to is your app store's purchase system, which on Google Play may also send Google its own diagnostics.
  • What you make stays with your family. Puppets and stories are never sent to us. They stay on your device, and in its own backup if you've switched backup on.
  • No camera, photos or microphone. BeddyPals never asks to use them, or your location or contacts.
  • Purchases go through your app store, behind a grown-up check.

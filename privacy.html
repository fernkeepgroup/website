BeddyPals Support Privacy

Privacy policy

BeddyPals is made for families with young children. This policy explains what BeddyPals keeps, where it keeps it, and what it never does, in the Amazon Appstore and Google Play versions, and the App Store version for iPhone and iPad. It also covers this website and email sent to our support addresses.

Effective date: October 15, 2026

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no accounts. The only outside service the app talks to is your app store's purchase system, which on Google Play may also send Google its own diagnostics.
  • What you make stays with your family. Puppets and stories are never sent to us. They stay on your device, and in its own backup if you've switched backup on.
  • No camera, photos or microphone. BeddyPals never asks to use them, or your location or contacts.
  • Purchases go through your app store, behind a grown-up check.

In detail

Who we are

BeddyPals is made by Fern Spark Studio, a trade name of Fernkeep Group LLC, a California company. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Fernkeep Group LLC. You can reach us about BeddyPals at beddypals@fernkeepgroup.com.

What BeddyPals keeps on your device

BeddyPals keeps its data in the app's own private storage on your device:

  • Settings and what you make: your preferences (such as display and sleep-timer choices, and where you left off), and the puppets and stories you create.
  • Purchase record: what your app store reports you have bought, so the app knows what is unlocked, and when it last checked. On Amazon devices this includes a scrambled version of the ID Amazon gives the app, used only to notice if a different Amazon account signs in. Amazon's purchase software in the app also keeps its own records there, including Amazon's account and receipt IDs. On Google Play and on iPhone and iPad, BeddyPals' record has no account ID at all. On Google Play, Google's billing library keeps its own files there too.

None of this is sent to us.

Puppets and stories you make

Puppets and stories you make stay on your device, and in your device's own backup if you've switched that on (see Backups). BeddyPals itself has no way to send, share or upload them, and no camera or photo access.

Backups

If you've switched on your device's own backup, it includes BeddyPals' settings and what your family made in the app: puppets, stories and drafts. That way they can come back on a new or reset device, or move with you when you set up a new one. Your purchases come from your app store, not from a backup: your store tells BeddyPals again what you own. On Amazon and Google Play devices, BeddyPals tells the backup to leave out its purchase record and the store software's own files. On an iPhone or iPad, the backup includes the app's record of what was unlocked, and the App Store's answer replaces it the next time the app checks.

  • Amazon Fire tablets: the tablet's own backup feature, where Amazon offers it, which keeps the backup with Amazon under Amazon's privacy notice.
  • Google Play: Android's own backup to your Google account, under Google's privacy policy.
  • iPhone and iPad: iCloud Backup, which keeps the backup with Apple under Apple's privacy policy, or a backup you make to your computer.

BeddyPals never sends a backup anywhere itself, and we never receive or see it. Backup is a setting of your device, not of the app: turning it off in your device's settings stops it. Uninstalling BeddyPals deletes its data from your device, but not from a backup already made; that goes when the backup does.

Permissions and the internet

BeddyPals never asks for the camera, photos, microphone, location or contacts, and its own code never connects to the internet, apart from links you tap that open in your browser. Your app store's purchase system does: when you buy something, and when the app checks what is unlocked. Everything else works offline.

On Google Play, Google's billing library adds the permissions it needs, including internet access, and may send Google its own diagnostics. What it sends, and how Google uses it, is covered by Google's privacy policy; none of it comes to us. These permissions don't show a prompt, and BeddyPals' own code doesn't use them.

On an iPhone or iPad, the plans screen, which only opens after the grown-up check, has two links that open in Safari: this policy, and Apple's standard license agreement for apps. BeddyPals has no other links, and on Amazon and Google Play devices it has none.

Purchases

Premium is bought through your app store, and only after a grown-up passes a short check designed for adults. Each store takes the payment and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Amazon Appstore: BeddyPals uses Amazon's Appstore SDK, which works through the Amazon Appstore app on your device. See Amazon's privacy notice for what Amazon collects.
  • Google Play: BeddyPals uses Google Play's billing library, which may also send Google its own diagnostics, as described above. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple's App Store (iPhone and iPad): BeddyPals uses Apple's in-app purchase system, which is part of iOS and iPadOS. Premium can be shared with your family through Family Sharing; with Ask to Buy, a child's purchase request goes to the family organizer; and Screen Time can turn in-app purchases off. See Apple's privacy policy.

Each app store also gives us its own reports about BeddyPals, such as how often it's downloaded and bought, and crash reports from people who chose in their device settings to share them with app developers. These come from the store, not from the app, and don't tell us who you or your child are.

Children's privacy

BeddyPals is made for a grown-up to use with a young child. We don't collect personal information from children, or from anyone, through BeddyPals. Nothing a child types, makes or taps in the app is sent to us. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and the app has no crash reporting of its own. Every purchase sits behind a grown-up check. On Google Play, Google's billing library may send Google its own diagnostics, as described above. Those are about billing, not about what a child does in the app.

Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no information about your child from the app. If you've emailed us, that email is the only thing we have, and we'll delete it on request. Everything BeddyPals keeps stays on your device, and in its own backup if you've switched that on. You can remove it from the device at any time by uninstalling the app, and from a backup through your device's backup settings. If you have questions about your child's privacy, email us.

This website

We don't run ads or analytics on fernkeepgroup.com and don't set cookies ourselves. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy policy for how it handles that information. Because the site doesn't track visitors, it works the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Email

If you email us (at beddypals@fernkeepgroup.com or support@fernkeepgroup.com), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply. Our email runs on Google Workspace. We keep messages only as long as we need them to help you, and we'll delete yours if you ask. If an email looks like it came from a child, we answer it once and then delete it, and we don't use it to contact them again. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also ask us for a copy of any email you've sent us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how BeddyPals or this website handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a future version of BeddyPals ever starts collecting information, that version will tell you in the app before it does.
